💡 When running remember to:

  1. Monitor for the following symptoms:
    • Urinary or fecal incontinence
    • Perineal, vaginal or lower abdominal heaviness
    • Urinary urgency
    • Recurring vaginal bleeding
  2. Do not squeeze core muscles during exercise — they contract naturally.
  3. Breastfeed before your workout.
  4. Adjust workouts based on comfort and symptoms:
    • Continue previous workouts
    • Repeat the same day or week instead of progressing
    • Replace with impact exercises (running on the spot)
    • Skip one workout, but never more than 3 days without running
  5. Seek help from a physiotherapist if you have questions or concerns.
